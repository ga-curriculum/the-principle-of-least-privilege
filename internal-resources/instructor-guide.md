## Intro to the Principle of Least Privilege exercise

For the exercise in the intro to the principle of least privilege lesson, students will be asked to think through a hypothetical data breach scenario and how the principle of least privilege could have prevented it. Here are some potential answers to consider:

### What went wrong

- Unnecessary admin access.
- No access time limits.
- No context-based restrictions.
- Poor access review process.

### How the principle of least privilege could have prevented this

- Intern would have limited, supervised access.
- Admin tasks would require an elevation process.
- System would flag unusual access patterns.
- Damage would be contained to one area.
